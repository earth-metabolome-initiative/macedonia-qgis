The field research will be 7-11 August. The activities will take place in the vicinity of Popova Shapka (42.015990, 20.881736) on Shar Planina mountain, which is also National Park - NP Shar Planina. In this period the fieldwork will cover the ranges from Popova Shapka (PS) to peak Titov (41.9922222, 20.7977778) and Ceripashina (42.0100869, 20.8523069) (Titov vrv, https://www.wikiloc.com/hiking-trails/popova-shapka-titov-vrv-1745074), Plat (42.0240438, 20.8132520), PS to Leshnica (https://www.wikiloc.com/hiking-trails/gorna-dolna-leshnica-popova-shapka-tetovo-macedonia-9783883) and PS to Uliverica (41.991621, 20.862261). As orientation, those would be the ranges in which we will move and do the research. The links from wikiloc and the coordinates are for easier orientation. Other notable locations which are used for orientation in this area, and which are in these ranges, are Jelak (42.024683, 20.872809) and peak Crn Vrv (42.0432176, 20.8626499). We will make the plan for each day  according to the interests.
Regarding the info for the students, they are undergraduate enthusiasts which will engage in the activities and not all of them have the ORCID/iNaturalist data. Our students enroll in such activities often, and some even have several years of experience in the areas and activities that we will cover, in which I believe they will rise to the challenge and also have great benefit.
